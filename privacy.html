Privacy Policy

Last updated September 6, 2026

The short version. This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and uses no advertising or tracking of any kind. It has no database and no server-side code — there is nowhere for it to store anything about you. The contact form does not submit to us; it opens a message in your own email program, which you choose whether to send. Every font, style, and script is served from our own domain, so loading a page here contacts no third party at all. We never sell, rent, or share your information.

Who we are

The FDC Foundation is the fundraising program supporting Friend de Coup, the show choir of Mitchell High School in Mitchell, South Dakota. We operate under the Mitchell Music Boosters, a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (EIN 83-2182557), PO Box 522, Mitchell, SD 57301. The Boosters are the legal organization behind this website and behind every donation made to the Foundation.

This policy explains what happens to information when you visit www.fdc-foundation.org or contact us. It covers this website only.

Information you choose to give us

We only receive information you deliberately send. There is no way for this site to collect anything from you passively.

  • The contact form. When you fill in the form on our home page and click send, the website does not transmit your details to us or to anyone else. It assembles your name, email address, selected topic, and message into a pre-written email and opens it in your own email program. Nothing leaves your device unless you then press send yourself. If you close that message instead, we never see it.
  • Email and phone. If you email or call us, we receive whatever you choose to tell us, held in our ordinary email account and phone records.
  • Donations. Gift details are handled by our payment processor, not by this website. See Donations and payment processing below.

We use this information only to reply to you and to carry out what you asked for. We do not add you to a mailing list without your say-so, and we do not sell, rent, trade, or share it with anyone outside the Foundation and the Mitchell Music Boosters.

Information collected automatically

We run no analytics and set no cookies, and this site loads nothing from a third party — fonts, styles, and scripts are all served from our own domain. One party does still see technical information, simply because a website has to be hosted somewhere:

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site. Like any web host, GitHub's servers record standard request data — including your IP address, browser type, and the pages requested — in security and operational logs. We do not have access to these logs and cannot identify visitors from them. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.

That is the complete list. The typefaces used in our design are stored on and served from this website rather than loaded from Google Fonts, so no request for them reaches Google or anyone else. Loading a page here contacts no company other than our host.

What this site does not do

  • No cookies, local storage, or session storage of any kind.
  • No analytics — no Google Analytics, no visitor counters, no heatmaps.
  • No advertising, no advertising networks, no retargeting, no tracking pixels.
  • No social media trackers or embedded "like" buttons.
  • No third-party fonts, scripts, or stylesheets — everything is served from our own domain.
  • No accounts, no logins, no passwords.
  • No collection of payment card numbers, bank details, or government identifiers.
  • No selling, renting, or trading of anyone's information, ever.

Donations and payment processing

Donations are made to the Mitchell Music Boosters and restricted to the Friend de Coup program. When online giving is active, payments are processed by Givebutter, a third-party donation platform. Clicking a donate button takes you to Givebutter's own secure pages, and everything you enter there — including card details — goes to Givebutter, not to us.

This website never receives, processes, or stores payment card information. From Givebutter we receive only what a nonprofit needs in order to thank you and issue your receipt: typically your name, email address, mailing address, gift amount, and any note you include. We keep donation records as long as required for the Boosters' accounting and IRS reporting obligations. Your use of Givebutter is governed by Givebutter's Privacy Policy.

If you would rather not use an online processor at all, you are welcome to mail a check payable to Mitchell Music Boosters with “FDC Foundation” on the memo line to PO Box 522, Mitchell, SD 57301.

Students, photographs, and children's privacy

This site is intended for a general audience — donors, alumni, families, and community supporters. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

This website features photographs of student performers. These are published with the permission arrangements of Mitchell High School and the Mitchell Music Boosters, and we identify students by name only where that permission covers it.

If you are a student, parent, or guardian and want a photograph removed, email us and we will take it down. You do not need to give a reason, and we will not ask for one. Requests are handled promptly — this matters more to us than any particular image does.

How we protect information

The site is served entirely over an encrypted HTTPS connection. Because the site holds no database, no accounts, and no visitor records, there is no store of personal information here to be breached. Information you send us by email is held in our email account, protected by the security of that provider, and access is limited to the Foundation committee members and Boosters officers who need it.

No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security — but we have deliberately built this site to hold as little as possible, which is the most reliable protection available.

Your choices

  • Ask what we hold. Email us and we will tell you what information about you we have, if any.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it. We will honor reasonable requests, subject to any donation records the Boosters must retain for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Ask us to stop contacting you. Say the word and we will.
  • Request removal of a photograph. See the section above.
  • Browse without being seen by third parties. Nothing further is required — this site loads no third-party resources, so no outside company learns that you visited.

Links to other sites

This site links to other organizations — among them Givebutter, Mitchell School District, and the Mitchell Area Performing Arts Center. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of any site we link to, and we encourage you to read the privacy policy of any site you visit from here.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if our practices change — most likely when online giving goes live or if we ever add a service to the site. The “last updated” date at the top always reflects the current version. Material changes will be noted plainly rather than slipped in quietly.

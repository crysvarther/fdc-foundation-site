Privacy Policy

Last updated November 21, 2026

The short version. We will never sell donor information, or any other personal information, to any other party. This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising. Donations are processed by Givebutter, not by this site, and we use what donors share with us only to thank them, issue receipts, keep the Foundation's records, and carry out their gift.

Who we are

The FDC Foundation (“the Foundation,” “we,” “us”) is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization in Mitchell, South Dakota, supporting Friend de Coup, the show choir of Mitchell High School. This policy explains what happens to information when you visit www.fdc-foundation.org, contact us, or give to the Foundation.

We do not sell your information

The FDC Foundation will not sell, rent, or trade donor information, or the personal information of anyone who visits this site or contacts us, to any other party. Ever. We do not share donor lists with other organizations, marketers, or businesses.

We share information only in these narrow cases: with the service providers that help us receive and record gifts (such as Givebutter, described below) and only so they can do that job; where the law requires it, such as tax or government reporting; or with your permission.

Information you choose to give us

  • Donations. When you give, we receive the details a nonprofit needs to thank you and issue a receipt: typically your name, email address, mailing address, gift amount and frequency, and any note or dedication you include. See Donations and payment processing below.
  • The contact form. The form on our home page does not send your details to a server. It assembles your name, email address, topic, and message into a pre-written email and opens it in your own email program. Nothing is sent unless you press send yourself.
  • Email and phone. If you email or call us, we receive whatever you choose to tell us.

We use this information to reply to you, to thank and recognize our supporters, to issue receipts, to keep the Foundation's financial and donor records, and to let you know how your gift is helping students. We will not add you to a mailing list without your say-so, and every message we send you will let you opt out.

Donations and payment processing

The Foundation receives online donations through Givebutter, a third-party donation platform. Clicking a donate button takes you to Givebutter's own secure pages, and everything you enter there, including payment card details, goes to Givebutter, not to this website. This website never receives, processes, or stores payment card information.

Givebutter collects and handles your information under its own privacy policy, which we encourage you to read. We keep the donor records we receive for as long as needed for the Foundation's accounting, tax, and legal obligations. Donor records are held in the Foundation's Givebutter account and our own files, and access is limited to the people who need it to run the Foundation.

If you would rather not use an online processor, you are welcome to give by check. Email info@fdc-foundation.org or call us and we will tell you where to send it.

Recognizing donors

Recognizing supporters, for example through giving levels or the Hall of Fame, is part of what the Foundation does. We will confirm with you before publishing your name, and you may choose to give anonymously or ask us to remove or change how your name appears at any time.

Information collected automatically

This site sets no cookies, uses no analytics (no Google Analytics, visitor counters, or heatmaps), and runs no advertising, retargeting, or tracking pixels. Two outside parties still receive basic technical information when you load a page, simply because of how websites are built:

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site. Like any web host, GitHub records standard request data, such as your IP address, browser type, and pages requested, in its security and operational logs. We do not have access to those logs. See GitHub's Privacy Statement.
  • Google Fonts supplies the typefaces used in our design, so your browser requests them from Google's servers, which receive your IP address and browser details. See Google's Privacy Policy.

Students and children's privacy

This site is intended for donors, alumni, families, and community supporters. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

This website features photographs of student performers. If you are a student, parent, or guardian and want a photograph removed, email us and we will take it down. You do not need to give a reason.

How we protect information

This site is served over an encrypted HTTPS connection, and it has no database, accounts, or logins of its own. Donor information held by the Foundation is kept in our donation platform account and our own files, with access limited to those who need it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to keep what we hold to a minimum and to protect it.

Your choices

  • Ask what we hold. Email us and we will tell you what information about you we have, if any.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it. We will honor reasonable requests, subject to donation records we must keep for tax and accounting purposes.
  • Ask us to stop contacting you. Tell us and we will.
  • Give anonymously or ask us not to list your name publicly.
  • Request removal of a photograph. See the section above.

Links to other sites

This site links to other organizations, including Givebutter. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of any site we link to, and we encourage you to read the privacy policy of any site you visit from here.

Changes to this policy

We will update this page if our practices change. The “last updated” date at the top always reflects the current version. Our promise not to sell donor information will not be removed.

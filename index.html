A Premiere Program

Mitchell's stage. A national reputation.

Friend de Coup doesn't just compete — it sets the standard. Grand Champion honors, a legacy Hall of Fame, and one of the region's marquee events, all run out of Mitchell.

Grand Champion titles FDC Hall of Fame Host of the Show Choir Classic

Each year FDC welcomes show choirs from across the country to the Mitchell Show Choir Classic — packing the house, filling downtown, and putting Mitchell on the map as a destination for the art form. It's competition, community, and hometown pride all at once.

The Legacy

"Friends take over."

The name Friend de Coup — loosely, "friends take over" — was adopted in memory of group member Jason Kaemingk, who passed away in 1995.

For more than 40 years it has stood for music, energy, camaraderie, and a standard of performance that defines Mitchell. Today FDC performs at the Mitchell Area Performing Arts Center and competes across the country — and the Foundation is how that tradition reaches the students who haven't yet stepped on stage.

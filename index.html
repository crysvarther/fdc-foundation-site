Our Mission

A foundation built so every student can take the stage.

The Friend de Coup Foundation exists for one reason: to make sure the experience that has shaped thousands of Mitchell students keeps thriving — independent of budget cuts and unaffected by a family's ability to pay.

Show choir is athletics, theater, and music rolled into one. It teaches discipline, teamwork, and the courage to perform. But excellence at the national level is expensive: choreographers, custom costumes, a live combo, set construction, sound, lighting, and the buses and hotels that carry the team to competition. The Foundation closes that gap so the program never has to choose between ambition and access.

We are powered entirely by donors who believe in what this program does for kids — alumni, parents, local businesses, and neighbors who have stood and cheered for Friend de Coup.

Our Legacy

"Friends take over."

The name Friend de Coup — loosely, "friends take over" — was adopted in memory of group member Jason Kaemingk, who passed away in 1995. For more than 40 years it has stood for music, energy, camaraderie, and a standard of performance that defines Mitchell.

  • 1980s

    A program is born

    Mitchell High School launches a show choir that quickly becomes a point of community pride and a proving ground for young performers.

  • 1995

    A name with meaning

    The group is renamed Friend de Coup in memory of member Jason Kaemingk — a tribute to friendship that still anchors the program's spirit.

  • Today

    Grand Champions, again and again

    Friend de Coup performs at the Mitchell Area Performing Arts Center and competes across the country, bringing home Grand Champion honors and unforgettable seasons.

  • Next

    The next 40 years — with you

    Donor support through the Foundation is what carries this tradition forward for the students who haven't yet stepped on stage.

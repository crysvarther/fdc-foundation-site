Our Mission

Built so every student can take the stage.

The FDC Foundation exists to make sure the experience that has shaped thousands of Mitchell students keeps thriving — for every kid, regardless of a family's ability to pay.

Show choir is athletics, theater, and music rolled into one. It teaches discipline, teamwork, and the courage to perform. But excellence at the competitive level is expensive: choreography, custom costumes, a live combo, sets, sound, lighting, and the buses and hotels that carry the team to competition.

Your support directly impacts students by removing financial barriers and expanding access to life-changing opportunities in the performing arts. We are powered entirely by donors who believe in what this program does for kids.

Our Legacy

"Friends take over."

The name Friend de Coup — loosely, "friends take over" — was adopted in memory of group member Jason Kaemingk, who passed away in 1995. For more than 40 years it has stood for music, energy, camaraderie, and a standard of performance that defines Mitchell.

Today Friend de Coup performs at the Mitchell Area Performing Arts Center and competes across the country, bringing home Grand Champion honors and unforgettable seasons. The Foundation is how that tradition reaches the students who haven't yet stepped on stage.

The full ensemble, mid-show
  • 1980s

    A program is born

    Mitchell High School launches a show choir that quickly becomes a point of community pride and a proving ground for young performers.

  • 1995

    A name with meaning

    The group is renamed Friend de Coup in memory of member Jason Kaemingk — a tribute to friendship that still anchors the program's spirit.

  • Today

    Grand Champions, again and again

    Friend de Coup competes across the country, bringing home top honors and giving 50+ students the experience of a lifetime each season.

  • Next

    The next chapter — with you

    Donor support through the FDC Foundation is what carries this tradition forward for the students of tomorrow.

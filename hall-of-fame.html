Coming to the Performing Arts Center lobby

A permanent home for 41 seasons.

Every Friend de Coup performer, director, volunteer, and champion belongs to the same story — and right now that story lives in scrapbooks and memories. The FDC Hall of Fame wall puts it where it belongs: on the wall of the theater where it happened.

The Hall of Fame

The people who built it.

Four decades of Friend de Coup have produced Grand Champion seasons, a home-grown invitational, and hundreds of students who found their footing on that stage.

The Hall of Fame honors the performers, directors, and volunteers behind all of it — inducted in classes, one group at a time, so the roll keeps growing as long as the program does. The wall gives every one of those names a place a family can stand in front of on a show night and point to.

The Wall

Three directions, one wall.

Twenty-four feet of the Performing Arts Center lobby, three ways to fill it. Nothing is built yet — this is the part where you get a say.

See how each wall reads once the lobby goes dark
Direction one

The Laurel

The classic hall-of-fame look. Cast letters across the top, the emblem lit at the center, and matched grids of engraved brass either side — with the founding donor band running along the bottom. Formal, symmetrical, and unmistakably permanent.

  • 48 name plates, individually replaceable
  • Room for the next 25 years of inductees
  • Founding donors recognized by giving level
Direction two

The Season Line

One line running the whole wall, with a mark for every season since 1985 and a gold star on every champion year. Hall of Fame classes hang above it at the year they were inducted. The line points off the end of the wall on purpose — the story isn't finished.

  • Every season on the wall, 1985 to today
  • Champion years called out in gold
  • Next season is a new mark, not a redesign
Direction three

Curtain Call

The dancers from the Hall of Fame emblem, cut from steel and standing off a lit panel so they throw a real shadow. Names live alongside on engraved brass ribbons, one per induction class, with the next one already waiting. This is the wall students stop and photograph.

  • Backlit — it comes alive after the house lights drop
  • A ribbon for every induction class
  • Built from our own emblem artwork
On the wall

Three kinds of names.

Whichever direction wins, the wall carries the same story.

Inductees

Performers, directors, and volunteers, honored in classes — each on their own engraved plate so the roll can grow every year.

Champion seasons

Every Grand Champion year marked permanently, so a season nobody wrote down still gets remembered in twenty years.

Founding donors

The people and families who made the wall possible, recognized by giving level right on it — the first names to go up.

Be on it

Help us put 41 seasons on the wall.

Founding donors are recognized on the wall itself. Have a favorite of the three? Tell us — the Committee is choosing a direction now.

What your gift does

Real impact, by the dollar.

  • $50 — Friend

    Helps outfit a performer with show-ready essentials.

  • $250 — Spotlight

    Covers a student's travel for a competition weekend.

  • $1,000 — Legacy

    Underwrites costumes and production for a number.

  • $5,000+ — Benefactor

    Seeds the endowment that sustains the program for years.
